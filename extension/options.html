★JobScout — first-time setup
  1. Sign in to JobScout at the URL you deployed (e.g. https://jobscout-wnwafxvfia-uw.a.run.app).
  2. Open Settings → Browser extension and click Generate token.
  3. Copy the jbsk_… token — it's only shown once.
  4. Paste it below and click Save.

Then visit any Upwork job page and press Alt+Shift+J.
